# Audio Mastering & Loudness Standardization v1.0

Rules:
- Target Loudness: -14.0 LUFS (Broadcast standard for YouTube & Web video).
- Peak Limiting: True Peak ceiling at -1.0 dBFS to prevent clipping distortion.
- Fade In / Fade Out: 0.5s smooth crossfade at scene boundaries.
- Export Formats: High-quality MP3 (320kbps) and uncompressed WAV (24-bit 44.1kHz).
