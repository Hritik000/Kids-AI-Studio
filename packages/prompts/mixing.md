# Intelligent Audio Mixing Directives v1.0

Rules:
1. Voice Narration: Primary channel at 0dB (100% volume).
2. Background Music: Automatically duck volume by -12dB (25% volume) during speech narration.
3. Ambient Audio: Secondary background layer at -18dB (15% volume).
4. Sound Effects: Accent layer at -6dB (50% volume).
5. Stereo Pan: Center voice narration, widen music and ambience across stereo field.
