# Camera Motion Directives v1.0

Supported Camera Paths:
- Static Shot: Fixed camera position with subtle subject parallax
- Slow Zoom In: Gentle push in towards character facial focal point
- Slow Zoom Out: Reveal full surrounding environment
- Pan Left / Pan Right: Smooth horizontal camera tracking
- Tilt Up / Tilt Down: Vertical sweep following character height or sky
- Tracking Shot: Dynamic side-by-side motion following walking/running hero
- Orbit Shot: 360-degree rotation around primary character
