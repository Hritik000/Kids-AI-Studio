# Character Motion & Expression Directives v1.0

Supported Character Animations:
- Idle Animation: Soft breathing, head tilting, natural eye blinking
- Walking / Running: Rhythmic leg movement, swinging arms, joyful bouncing stride
- Jumping: Dynamic vertical lift with arms spreading happily
- Waving / Pointing: Hand motion towards camera or key objects
- Expressions: Happy smile, wide curious eyes, playful laughter, surprised gasped expression
