# Character & Environmental Continuity Prompt Module v1.0

Directive:
Audit and enforce strict visual continuity across all storyboard scenes:
1. Character features (skin tone, clothing, species, color highlights) MUST remain identical across scenes.
2. Environmental elements (sky color, background mountains, vegetation style) MUST match the master color palette.
3. Lighting style and time of day progression MUST remain logically consistent.
