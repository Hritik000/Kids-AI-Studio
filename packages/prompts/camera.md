# Camera Planning Prompt Module v1.0

Directive:
Define precise 3D camera language for each scene:
- Shot Type: Wide Shot, Medium Shot, Close Up, Extreme Close Up
- Angle: Low Angle, Eye Level, High Angle, Overhead
- Motion: Pan, Zoom, Tracking, Static
- Speed: Slow, Gentle, Energetic
- Transition: Cut, Fade, Cross Fade, Slide, Zoom, Match Cut
