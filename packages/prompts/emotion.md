# Emotional Vocal Tones v1.0

Supported Emotions:
- Happy: Bright, smiling vocal cadence, cheerful pitch
- Excited: Fast pacing, high energy, buoyant expression
- Curious: Rising intonation, questioning tone, slow inquisitive pause
- Surprised: Wide pitch jump, expressive gasp
- Calm / Gentle: Soft, reassuring, soothing rhythm for storytime
- Educational: Clear, deliberate articulation with friendly warmth
